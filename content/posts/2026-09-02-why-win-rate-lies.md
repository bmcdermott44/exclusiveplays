title: Why win rate is the stat that tells you the least
date: 2026-09-02
summary: A 60% bettor on heavy chalk loses money all year. Here is the arithmetic nobody selling picks wants to show you.
---
Every capper leads with win rate because it is the easiest number to make look good. Bet enough
heavy favourites and you can hit 65% and still finish the year down.

The price is what decides whether a win is worth anything. Laying -300 means four wins pay for one
loss. Taking +180 means one win pays for nearly two losses. Those are different jobs and the same
win rate describes both.

I hit well under half my plays. I am up because I take prices other people will not, and because
every bet is graded whether it wins or not.
